For Raima

There are some things I’ve been too afraid to say...

Hi Raima.

I don’t know where to start, Raima, but the girl I’ve been talking about is you. Probably you know it too.

It all started back in September 16th, 2025. I was lonely, kinda broken, and my mind wasn’t feeling that good. Then you came. I texted you first—I wasn’t going to text you, but Mahee insisted. Maybe that was the biggest assist he ever did.

For the first time ever, I got someone to share my problems with, someone who wouldn’t scold me for my mistakes, rather listen to them, support me when I needed it the most, someone who’d care about me unconditionally. Maybe that was the reason why I ranked in the pre-test. Jokes aside, I never felt that kind of caring and motivation from someone else before.

I got proposals from four different girls, but I don’t want them. I don’t want any other girls. You’re the first and might be the last. Even if you don’t put in efforts, I will. I will put my 100% into you. I know it’s a gamble—I’m putting my entire lifeline on someone like you. I’ve known you for about a year, but I feel like I’ve known you for my entire life.

Oh God, I love you so much. I always put you in my prayers. Believe me or not, I’ve never prayed for someone or something so deeply. You’re the one who kept my hope alive. You’re the most precious person in my life. I’ve never become this desperate for someone—you’re the first person I’ve been so desperate for, and might be the last.

But one day you ghosted me. That day my heart got shattered. I felt betrayed, I became so hopeless, and I never told anyone. But after six long months, you finally came back. I felt like the happiest person on earth. I was thanking God for at least having a friend like you—or so I thought.

The day that I met you, I started dreaming. Dreaming about every possibility, conversations we had, and a future where you’d be mine.

You became the person I cared about the most. You’re the embodiment of my dream partner, and I never wanna lose you.

I’ve put in so much effort. Maybe those weren’t enough, and maybe you don’t think the same. Maybe for you I am just another guy.

But you?

You’re my dream. You’re the person I asked God for. You’re the person I am so desperate to have. I care about you more than I know how to explain. I find myself thinking about you—whether you’re happy, whether you’ve had a good day, whether there’s something I could do to make things better.

I’ve always motivated you even when I wasn’t okay. I’ve always tried to understand you. That’s why you would find me knowing every bit of detail about you.

Listen, Raima. I don’t know if you’re going to accept me or not, but one thing I’m pretty sure of is that I’ll never love someone the way I love you.

I love you soooo much, Raima.

In ways I can’t tell.

At first, I wanted you to see me. I wanted you to care about me. Then my heart grew greedy. Now I want you to love me.

I’m jealous of the sun that gets to shine on your beautiful eyes every day. I’m jealous of the moon that gets to bathe you in its light. I’m jealous of the breeze that gets to touch a precious soul like you.

I’m jealous because the world gets to have you.

I’ve come to a conclusion: whether I’m winning with you by my side or I’m winning alone, winning isn’t an option anymore—it’s my promise.

I don’t know how to handle a rejection, but I surely know how to love a person like you.

Raima, I love you in ways I can’t explain. I’ve always wanted to say those words, but I was afraid—afraid that things might go wrong.

But now I know pretty well that you’ll be my first and last love of my life.

And about the whole admission thing, I must win. There’s no option, and I will. I promise. I’ll make you the happiest person on earth in ways you’d never imagine in a thousand years.

One thing I forgot to mention: I had a gift for you which has been sitting on my desk for the past three months.

Anyways, take care. Always take care of your parents.

And I LOVE YOU, RAIMA, WHETHER YOU LOVE ME BACK OR NOT.

I will never forget someone like you.

And shuno, dushtu meye—if you’re going to say no, please, no need for an explanation. Just say it right away. I’ll respect your answer, even if it hurts, because I’d rather know the truth than keep wondering.

I’ll always be grateful that I got to love someone with my whole heart.

- LOVE YOU FOREVER AND EVER, RAIMAA ❤️
